ABOUT SAKSHAM PATHSHALA

शिक्षा जो केवल पढ़ाए नहीं—सक्षम बनाए।

Saksham Pathshala is an initiative of Saksham — Samdrishti, Kshamata Vikas evam Anusandhan Mandal — created with a simple belief: every person has potential. Opportunity helps unlock it.

Explore the Path
OUR AIM

Make meaningful education more reachable.

Bring accessible online and offline learning, skill development, educational resources and opportunities for practical growth together for Divyangjan.

OUR BELIEF

Accessibility should be built in from the beginning.

We do not want accessibility to be something added at the end. We want it built into learning from the very beginning.

THE MODEL

A connected learning ecosystem.

Education, resources, live learning, skills and practical exposure should feel connected.
